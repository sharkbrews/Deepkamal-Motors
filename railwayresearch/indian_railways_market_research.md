# Indian Railways: Market Entry Research for Hydraulic Equipment Suppliers

**Purpose:** Help a hydraulic seal-kit and spare-parts business (JCB parts background) understand how Indian Railways is organised, where hydraulic jacks, pumps and related products are bought and used, and who to approach.
**Research date:** 8 October 2026
**How to read confidence tags:**
- `[SRC]` = taken from a web source checked during this research (listed in Section 14)
- `[KNOW]` = general domain knowledge, not re-verified. Confirm before relying on it for a business decision.

> **Note for the dashboard / website builder:** Sections 3, 4, 5, 6 and 9 are written as clean tables so they can be turned directly into maps, filters and org-chart visuals. Section 13 has suggested dashboard modules and data schemas.

## 1. Executive Summary

1. **Indian Railways has 18 zones** as of 1 June 2026, when South Coast Railway (HQ Visakhapatnam) began working as the 18th zone. `[SRC]` Counts of 17 or 19 still appear online because older lists are out of date or wrongly include Konkan Railway.
2. **There are about 70 divisions** (a count derived from official notices, not an official published total). `[SRC]` Divisions are where trains are actually run and maintained; zones are where management sits.
3. **Railways buy through a layered system:** Railway Board (policy) → Zonal Railways (General Manager, Principal Chief Materials Manager) → Divisions (DRM, Divisional Materials Manager) → depots, sheds, workshops. More than 250 stores depots serve the network. `[SRC]`
4. **Tendering is online.** The two main routes are **IREPS** (ireps.gov.in) and **GeM** (gem.gov.in). The Ministry of Railways has said railway-specific purchases will move to GeM. `[SRC]` Live tenders for hydraulic jacks already appear on IREPS (e.g., a Western Railway tender for a 50-ton hydraulic jack with traversing base, EMD about ₹4.6 lakh, closing June 2026). `[SRC]`
5. **Some items can only be bought from RDSO-approved sources.** RDSO (Research Designs & Standards Organisation, Lucknow) runs vendor approval. New vendors must register with it. RDSO also lists a set of items restricted to approved sources. `[SRC]`
6. **Best-fit product areas for a seal-kit and hydraulic parts business:**
   - Accident Relief Train (ART) and Accident Relief Medical Equipment (ARME) hydraulic re-railing equipment, telescopic jacks, power packs `[SRC]`
   - Coach, wagon and loco workshop lifting jacks and hydraulic tools `[KNOW]`
   - Track maintenance hydraulic jacks `[SRC for product existence]`
   - **Aftermarket seal kits and repair parts for hydraulic cylinders** on track machines, cranes and road-rail vehicles `[KNOW]`
7. **Recommended first move:** register on GeM and IREPS, get Udyam/MSME status, collect ISO and test documentation, then bid on small and medium jack and seal tenders at the zones and divisions closest to your base, while pursuing RDSO approval for controlled items.

## 2. How Indian Railways Is Organised (Hierarchy)

```
Ministry of Railways (Union Minister of Railways)
└── Railway Board (Chairman & CEO + Members)               ← policy, budgets, standards
    ├── Directorates (Stores, Mechanical Engg, Civil Engg, Electrical, etc.)
    ├── RDSO, Lucknow (standards, vendor approval)
    ├── Production Units (locomotive, coach, wheel factories)   ← report to Board directly
    ├── PSUs (DFCCIL, IRCON, RITES, etc.)
    └── 18 Zonal Railways
        └── General Manager (GM) per zone
            ├── Principal Heads of Departments (PHODs)
            │   PCMM (Materials/Stores), PCME (Mechanical), PCEE (Electrical),
            │   PCE (Civil), PCSTE (Signal & Telecom), PCOM (Operations), etc.
            ├── Workshops (headed by Chief Workshop Manager)
            └── Divisions (3 to 6 per zone)
                └── Divisional Railway Manager (DRM)
                    ├── Sr. DME / Sr. DEE / Sr. DEN / Sr. DSTE (department heads)
                    ├── Sr. DMM / DMM (Divisional Materials Manager, stores)
                    └── Depots, loco sheds, C&W depots, ART/ARME, track depots
```

**What this means in practice**

| Level | Role | Why it matters to a supplier |
|---|---|---|
| Railway Board | Sets policy, procurement rules, approves standards. Chairman & CEO is Satish Kumar, extended to 31 Aug 2027. `[SRC]` | Not a direct buyer for small items, but policy and specifications originate here. |
| Member (Traction & Rolling Stock) | Oversees production units, mechanical workshops, coaches, locomotives and materials management. `[SRC]` | Relevant authority for mechanical and stores policy. |
| Railway Stores Directorate | Additional Member (Railway Stores) sits under this member, with an Executive Director and Directors below. `[SRC]` | Sets stores and vendor registration rules. |
| Zonal Railway (GM) | Large purchases, vendor registration, workshops | Primary tender issuer. PCMM office runs most procurement. |
| Division (DRM) | Maintenance and day-to-day purchases for sheds and depots | Many smaller jack, tool and spare-part tenders come from here. |
| Workshops and depots | End users (carriage, wagon, loco, track, ART) | They define what is actually needed. Technical staff influence specifications. |

**Zone vs division in one line:** the zone is where Indian Railways is managed; the division is where trains are run. A GM heads a zone; a DRM heads a division. `[SRC]`

**Important caveat on titles:** Indian Railways merged its eight Group-A services into the Indian Railway Management Service (IRMS) and restructured the Board along functional lines in 2020. `[SRC]` Post titles such as PCMM and DRM continue to appear in current documents, but exact designations can shift. Always check the current officer directory on the zone website.

### Things that confuse people (useful for the dashboard tooltips)
- Zone headquarters are often **not** the state capital: East Central = Hajipur (not Patna), West Central = Jabalpur (not Bhopal), South East Central = Bilaspur (not Raipur), South Western = Hubballi (not Bengaluru). `[SRC]`
- Duplicate division names: **Nagpur** (Central and South East Central), **Lucknow** (Northern and North Eastern). `[SRC]`
- **Konkan Railway is not a zone.** It is a separate public sector company (Konkan Railway Corporation Ltd). `[SRC]`
- **South Coast Railway** is the newest zone. It was carved from South Central and East Coast Railways. Waltair division was split: Visakhapatnam division went to SCoR and a new Rayagada division went to East Coast. `[SRC]`
- **Count variations online:** Various sites say 17, 18 or 19 zones, and 68, 73 or 74 divisions. Cause: out-of-date lists, or counting Konkan Railway. The 18 / ~70 figures above follow the most recent notifications found.

**Real tender examples found `[SRC]`**
- Western Railway Stores: "Procurement of 50 Ton capacity Hydraulic Jack with Traversing base", open tender, two-stage bidding, EMD ₹4,59,690, closing 19 June 2026. The value of about ₹2.30 crore was *derived by an aggregator from the EMD*, not published by the railway.

**Standards to know:** A European standard exists for re-railing and recovery requirements for rail vehicles, covering lifting points and design load cases (it references lifting brackets in the 100 to 220 kN range). `[SRC]` Indian Railways follows its own RDSO specifications, so check RDSO for the specifications that apply to each item. ## 7. How Procurement Works

### 7.1 Channels

| Channel | What it is | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **IREPS** (ireps.gov.in) | Railways' own e-procurement system for goods, works and services, used by zones, divisions and PUs. `[SRC]` | Needs vendor registration and Digital Signature Certificate (DSC). `[SRC]` |
| **GeM** (gem.gov.in) | Government e-Marketplace. Ministry told vendors railway-specific items will be bought via GeM (CLW notice, Aug 2020). `[SRC]` | Free seller registration; includes bidding and reverse auctions. `[SRC]` |
| **Stock (stores) purchase** | Stores department handles procurement, storage and issue of stock items for all other departments. `[SRC]` | Common for consumable and repeat-use items like seal kits. |
| **Non-stock / user-department purchase** | Workshops and sheds buy items for specific jobs | Often via local tenders on IREPS/GeM. |
| **Limited / single tender** | Used for proprietary or RDSO-controlled items | Usually only approved vendors are invited. |

### 7.2 Vendor approval (important for jacks and similar equipment)

`[SRC]` The Ministry's RDSO master circular (June 2018) sets these principles:
- Railways keep lists of **approved vendors** for certain items. New or untried vendors approach RDSO for registration and approval.
- Vendors can start as **developmental vendors** and be upgraded to approved after satisfactory supply against a developmental order.
- Where **no approved vendor exists** for an item, developmental vendors can be used. Where there is **only one approved vendor**, developmental orders are possible.
- There is a defined list of items (the circular refers to 57 items controlled by RDSO's Materials & Chemical directorate) restricted to RDSO-approved sources.
- RDSO notes the **MSME status** of vendors in its vendor directory.
- Some zones also keep their own **registered vendor lists** for items that RDSO does not control.

**Action for you:** for each product on your list, search the RDSO vendor directory and the item's RDSO specification to see whether it is a controlled item, then decide between (a) local vendor registration with a zone/PU, (b) RDSO developmental registration, or (c) selling through an approved OEM.

### 7.3 Policy tailwinds for small suppliers `[KNOW]`
*(Verify current terms at msme.gov.in and gem.gov.in.)*
- **Public Procurement Policy for MSEs:** MSE vendors typically get EMD exemption, tender-fee waiver and reserved purchase share.
- **Make in India preference (PPP-MII):** local content requirements favour Indian manufacturers.
- **GeM** gives MSEs and startups easier access with fewer registration barriers. `[SRC]`

### 7.4 Documents typically needed `[KNOW]`
GST registration; PAN; Udyam (MSME) certificate; ISO 9001 certificate; GeM seller ID; DSC (digital signature); test certificates and drawings; past supply orders (to prove experience); bank solvency or turnover proof; OEM authorisation if you resell another brand; product catalogues with load ratings and test reports.

### 7.5 Decentralisation and why it matters
`[SRC]` A CAG report notes that railways decentralised procurement to zones and PUs, giving GMs power to buy many stores items directly. Practical effect: **there is no single national buyer. You must sell zone by zone and PU by PU**, though a registered vendor status at one place often helps credibility at another.

## 8. Opportunity Assessment for a Seal-Kit and Hydraulic Parts Business

### 8.1 Product-market fit (judgement, not sourced data)

| Product | Fit | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Seal kits for hydraulic jacks and cylinders** (ART jacks, track machines, cranes) | **Very high** | Matches existing core business. Railways run large installed bases of hydraulic equipment needing periodic overhaul. |
| **Hydraulic hand pumps and power packs** | High | Used with re-railing equipment and lifting sets. |
| **Telescopic and bottle jacks (5T to 50T+) incl. traversing base** | High, but spec-heavy | Live tenders exist. Expect strict specifications, warranty periods (e.g., 30 months seen) and test requirements. |
| **Synchronised lifting systems and re-railing sets** | Medium | High value but needs engineering capability and possibly OEM tie-up. |
| **Hoses, fittings, valves** | Medium-high | Frequently bought as stock items. |
| **Repair / refurbishment services for cylinders** | High | Workshops and track machine depots outsource this. |
| **Dampers, buffers and other rolling-stock components** | Low in the near term | Controlled approvals, OEM-driven. |

### 8.2 Entry routes, ranked by speed
1. **Tender bidding on GeM / IREPS** for stock items (seal kits, hoses, pumps, small jacks). Fastest but competitive (L1 pricing).
2. **Local vendor registration** with zonal PCMM office and nearby divisions or workshops.
3. **Supplier to track-machine OEMs and their Indian service partners** (aftermarket seals and repairs).
4. **RDSO development/approval** for jacks or other controlled items. Slower, but creates a durable moat.
5. **Partner with an established ART-equipment manufacturer** to supply components.

### 8.3 Risks and watch-outs
- **L1 (lowest price) bidding** pressure and delayed payments are typical in government procurement. `[KNOW]`
- **Specification mismatch:** tender specs are detailed and rejections for technical non-compliance are common. `[KNOW]`
- **Warranty obligations** can be long (30 months appears in one tender). `[SRC]`
- **Counterfeit and spurious parts** are a safety concern for jacks used at accident sites; buyers will want test certificates and traceability. `[KNOW]`
- **Zone reorganisation (South Coast Railway, Rayagada)** changes which office issues tenders in Andhra Pradesh, Odisha and nearby areas. Check current tender-issuing office names. `[SRC]`
- **Officer rotation** means relationships need re-building every few years. `[KNOW]`

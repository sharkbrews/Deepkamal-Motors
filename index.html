About Us

20+ Years of Industrial Excellence

Welcome to Deep Kamal Motors, Bhopal — your number one stop for all ranges of Hydraulic and Pneumatic Seals, Gears and Gear Parts, V-Belts, Earthmoving Pumps, Grease, Bearings, O-Rings and Backup Seals, Custom Made Seals, and Hydraulic & Pneumatic Cylinder Repairs.

Founded in 2004, Deepkamal Motors has come a long way from its beginnings. Today we serve customers all over Madhya Pradesh and its surrounding areas. You name it, we'll do it — with this motto in our heads, we serve all types of profiles and sizes of seals and gears. We do not believe in a set-it-and-forget-it system; our clients are our top priority. We are always striving to be better so our clients achieve more.
